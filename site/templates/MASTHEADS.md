# Singhasan mastheads

Both approved alternatives are retained at the user’s request.

- **A — Throne & earth:** `masthead-a.html`. Current masthead, selected after the full-page comparison on 4 October 2026.
- **B — Literary wordmark:** `masthead-b.html`. Retained alternative, with a leaf underline.

Both use selectable Odia lettering in the self-hosted Anek Odia 600 font. Variant styling is preserved in `site/dist/assets/visual-refinements.css` under `.brand-a` and `.brand-b`; the corresponding SVG marks are `masthead-throne.svg` and `masthead-leaf.svg`.

The builder’s `masthead_logo()` selects A. A future switch only requires selecting the other template and rebuilding. Keep both templates, marks and styles. The throne favicon is independent and stays unchanged. Noto Serif Oriya remains the poem-reading font.
