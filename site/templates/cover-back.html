Power passes.
The earth remembers.

A throne lies beneath an ordinary patch of earth. Above it, cattle graze, children gather, and life goes on.

Across sixty-eight poems, myth and history meet the ceremonies of power and the lives of ordinary people. The journey returns to the earth, where an old story may begin again.

ପ୍ରଭାକର ଶତପଥୀ

Pravakar Satapathy is an Odia poet and editor associated with Jajpur. His writing spans poetry and the literary culture of little magazines.
