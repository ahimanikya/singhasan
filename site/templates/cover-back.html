Power passes.
The earth remembers.

A throne lies beneath an ordinary patch of earth. Above it, cattle graze, children gather, and life goes on.

Sixty-eight poems follow power through myth, history and ordinary life, returning to the earth where an old story may begin again.

ପ୍ରଭାକର ଶତପଥୀ

Odia poet and editor associated with Jajpur. Singhasan is his first collection.
