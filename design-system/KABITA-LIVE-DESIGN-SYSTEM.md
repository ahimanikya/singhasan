# Kabita Live — design system

Design system by **Ahimanikya Satapathy**.

The journal should feel like cotton paper, a quiet room and a voice close enough to hear. Art creates atmosphere; poems, names and navigation stay clear. This document separates approved decisions from the component work still to review.

### A shared language, with room for each page

Build a quiet literary opening: a small artistic mark and concise label, a clear display heading, a short invitation, the existing page-specific watercolor and the compact wave-and-leaf ornament. Keep the text and artwork in a balanced desktop composition; the exact split follows the page template, not a forced universal ratio. Search, reading and submission actions must arrive promptly after the opening. Reduce redundant headings and stacked margins before reducing legibility or touch targets.

Reuse the approved fonts and palette. Cormorant Garamond carries English display headings and genuine italic quotations; Source Serif 4 carries sustained English prose; Noto Serif Oriya and Tiro Devanagari Hindi retain natural upright shaping. Use the approved sans serif for controls. A request for heavier type changes weight within these families, not the font. Cover-story titles use bold Cormorant Garamond with regular Source Serif 4 prose. Laterite gives emphasis to selected marks and quotations; ink carries the main text and muted ink supports metadata. Keep colors theme-aware.

Write poetic invitations where they help a reader enter the page, and plain labels where the reader must act. Use English poet display names consistently; retain original-script names in search data and original poem titles. Do not turn internal terms, source audits or workflow status into decorative page copy. Derive edition dates and counts from the catalogue rather than freezing them in prose.

### Watercolors, portraits and provenance

Opening illustrations, edition covers and poem art are borderless, with soft edges that meet the paper. The homepage’s fine warm keyline and the poet portrait’s paper frame are deliberate exceptions. Keep typography separate from art. Choose poem illustrations by the poem’s imagery and retain stable mappings; never borrow an edition cover for a poem or randomly rotate unrelated pictures. Preserve historical covers, original photographs, artwork masters and asset-specific requirements.

Poet watercolor portraits follow the editors’ artwork treatment: a realistic, recognizable face with delicate watercolor/graphite edges on ivory cotton paper and restrained teal, ochre and laterite washes. Preserve age, complexion, expression, hair, clothing, glasses, bindi and jewelry. The identified saved photograph is the identity source; an editor portrait is only a style reference. Visually compare before using a generated portrait, retain the original if material identity drift cannot be resolved, and record uncertainty for blurred sources. Missing-photo profiles keep initials. The central portrait mapping supplies profile artwork and circular thumbnails. Read `kb/research/writers/portrait-batches.json` for progress; do not equate a style approval or batch completion with all portraits being finished.

Generic generated-art and translation disclosure, design/font credits, portrait sources and hosting credit belong in Our Story → Credits & colophon. Poet bylines, named translator credits and quoted-work citations remain beside their content. Keep image captions/narratives in their provenance records even where a reader placement omits the visible caption. No extra art caption belongs beneath poem artwork or beside an already narrated edition cover. Asset-specific attribution commitments take precedence over generic placement.

### Reading space and responsive behavior

At 760px and below, inner-page opening illustrations, edition covers and poem artwork fill the available content width inside the existing gutters. Preserve natural aspect ratios without clipping or stretching. This supersedes earlier hidden directory artwork and small 170/225px mobile illustration caps. Portraits, tiny icons and footer signatures follow their own sizing rules, not the full-width illustration selector.

Use compact related groups and a clear path to reading. On Poems, retain 12px bottom padding and 12px bottom margin at the opening. Search uses one desktop row; on mobile the keyword field spans a row above the two choices. Labels are 13px and inputs 48px high. Reader-panel labels are 12px; keep inputs at readable sizes and controls at least 44px. Mobile poem/edition rows and contribution tables become cards while desktop retains its appropriate table/list structure. Keep English poet names and a single “Original: Odia/Hindi/English” context where required, without repeating “Original” throughout each card.

Aa contains Original, Odia, Hindi and English plus Standard, Large and Extra large text. Original resolves to the actual source language; unavailable versions stay unavailable. Preserve genuine translator credits and fallback behavior. Dismiss popovers with outside click, Escape or their close control. Quiet-reader footer numbering shows only the current page or spread (1, or 1–2), without totals or a poem counter; page regions retain descriptive accessible labels. Keep saved marks and bookmarks device-local; underlining remains a selection feature, without a persistent toolbar U icon.

Quiet reading removes website navigation and footer decoration. Edition entry preselects that edition; Poems entry starts with the full collection. The Aa collection chooser can select an edition or poet, search, Read poem or Read all; do not silently equate a regular-page search filter with the active quiet-reader collection. Flow poems continuously: fit the title/byline/ornament and at least three opening verse lines before starting a new poem in remaining space, or move the opening to the next page. Preserve source lineation and stanza structure. Keep optional page-turn animation, reduced-motion behavior and soft paper sound off by default. Recited poem audio remains deferred. Physical Apple trackpad/touch and sound audition are distinct from browser checks.

The mobile menu remains a focused drawer with generous targets, clear dismissal, focus restoration and scroll locking. The shared direct theme control cycles System → Light → Dark and remembers the choice; no extra theme dropdown. Preserve night-reading contrast and texture rules.
